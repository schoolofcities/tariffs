# Guideline for developers / contributors to this project

This document serves as a guide for those looking to contribute to any part of this project.

These are the main components and pages:

## Direct exposure
- map is the direct exposure map of the HS codes to Canadian businesses
- cma-ranking-charts is the cma level aggregate analysis of the dissemination areas' direct exposure of businesses, employees (place) and employees (work)
- potential-local-impacts is an article on the first iteration of the tariffs website (October 2025)
- section-338, lumber, trucks are articles about their respective tariffs shortly after they were implemented

- retaliatory-effects is an experimental page for how Canadian retaliatory tariffs have affected U.S. businesses

## Canada U.S. visits
- canada-us-visits is the main page for the decline in Canadian visits to the U.S.

- canada-us-regression is in development
- canada-us-visits-sep is in development

## Scenarios
- map-scenarios maps out the scenarios given by Rick's analysis
- map-scenarios-charts shows the cma and province level aggregate analysis of Rick's analysis
